European Digital Evidence Network

Building shared understanding of digital evidence

A European network connecting law, forensic science and criminal justice practice.

EDEN is a proposal for interdisciplinary cooperation on how digital evidence is identified, handled and assessed across European jurisdictions.

The initiative

About EDEN

Digital evidence often crosses borders, while the legal rules and technical practices used to evaluate it differ between jurisdictions.

The European Digital Evidence Network (EDEN) proposes a sustained forum for legal scholars, judges, prosecutors, defence practitioners, forensic scientists and technologists to compare approaches and exchange expertise.

The proposal focuses on the relationship between the reliability of digital evidence and the procedural safeguards that protect the rights of everyone involved in criminal proceedings.

Purpose

Objectives

01

Compare legal and technical approaches to digital evidence across European jurisdictions.

02

Identify differences, challenges and practices that can inform more consistent procedures.

03

Examine how procedural rules and technical standards support integrity and authentication.

04

Support knowledge exchange and capacity building for justice and forensic professionals.

05

Develop shared principles and recommendations for cross-border cooperation.

Research themes

Work areas

WORK AREA 01

Legal categories and definitions

How jurisdictions classify digital evidence, including metadata, cloud-stored material and live data.

WORK AREA 02

Safeguards and admissibility

Procedural protections, admissibility standards, due process and defence rights.

WORK AREA 03

Cross-border access and cooperation

Legal mechanisms and practical challenges when evidence or service providers span jurisdictions.

WORK AREA 04

Forensic integrity and reliability

Chain of custody, validation, reproducibility and the assessment of forensic methods.

WORK AREA 05

Training and knowledge exchange

Capacity building and the exchange of knowledge among judicial, legal and technical communities.
